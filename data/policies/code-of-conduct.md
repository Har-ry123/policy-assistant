## Respect at work
Acme has zero tolerance for harassment and discrimination.

## Reporting concerns
Report concerns to your manager, HR, or the anonymous Ethics Hotline at 1800-555-0199. Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is prohibited.

## Gifts
Do not accept gifts worth more than $50 from vendors or clients. Disclose any gift above $25 to your manager.

## Conflict of interest
Disclose any conflict of interest, such as a family member working for a vendor, to HR in writing.

## Confidentiality
Do not share confidential company information on social media.
