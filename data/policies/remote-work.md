## Hybrid model
Acme follows a hybrid model: employees work from the office 3 days a week. Tuesday to Thursday are the core office days.

## Working from home
Employees may work from home up to 2 days a week after informing their manager.

## Core hours
Core working hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time.

## Working from another country
Working from another country is allowed for up to 20 days per year. It needs written approval from HR and the IT security team, and it is only allowed in countries on the approved list.

## Home office equipment
A one-time home-office stipend of $300 is paid for a desk or chair. Home internet is reimbursed at $40 per month.
