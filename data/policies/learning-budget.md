## Yearly budget
Each employee has a learning budget of $1,200 per year for courses, books and conferences.

## Unused budget
Unused budget does not roll over to the next year.

## Approval
Purchases above $500 need your manager's approval.

## Repayment
If you leave within 12 months of a course that cost more than $1,000, you must repay the cost pro rata.
