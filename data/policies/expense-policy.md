## Submitting claims
Submit expense claims within 30 days of the expense in the finance portal. Receipts are required for every item above $25.

## Meal limits
Meals are reimbursed up to $50 per day for domestic travel and $75 per day for international travel. Alcohol is never reimbursed.

## Approval levels
Claims up to $500 are approved by your manager. Claims above $500 need director approval. Claims above $5,000 need CFO approval.

## Payment time
Approved claims are paid within 10 working days.

## Mileage
Using a personal car for business is reimbursed at $0.50 per kilometre.
