## Review cycle
Performance reviews are held twice a year, in June and December.

## Ratings
Ratings use a scale of 1 to 5, where 5 is the highest.

## Feedback
You receive written feedback within 10 working days after your review meeting.

## Appeals
To appeal a rating, write to HR within 14 days of receiving it.
