## Data breaches
Report a suspected data breach to privacy@acme.example within 24 hours.

## Deletion requests
Customer requests to delete personal data must be answered within 30 days.

## Screen lock
Lock your screen after 5 minutes of inactivity.

## Record retention
Finance records are kept for 7 years.

## Classification
Company data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Never email Restricted data outside the company.
