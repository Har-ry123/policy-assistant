## Annual leave
Every full-time employee receives 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues at 2 days per month.

## Carry forward
Up to 5 unused days can be carried forward into the next year. Carried-forward days must be used by 31 March, otherwise they lapse.

## Sick leave
Employees get 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required when sick leave lasts more than 2 consecutive days.

## Requesting leave
Submit leave requests in the HR portal. Requests of up to 3 days need 2 working days of notice. Longer requests need 5 working days of notice. Requests longer than 10 consecutive days also need HR approval.

## Parental leave
Maternity leave is 26 weeks of paid leave. Paternity leave is 2 weeks of paid leave.

## Bereavement leave
Employees may take 5 days of paid bereavement leave for the death of an immediate family member.
