## Emergencies
In an emergency, call the security desk on extension 100.

## Fire drills
Fire drills are held every quarter. The fire assembly point is Car Park B.

## Injuries
Report any injury within 24 hours.

## Working late
Employees working after 21:00 can request a company cab. Request it by 18:00.
