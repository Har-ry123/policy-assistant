## First day
On day one you receive a laptop and access to the GitHub organisation.

## Code review
Every pull request needs at least 1 approval before merging.

## Deployments
Production deploys are frozen on Fridays after 15:00.

## On-call
Engineers join the on-call rotation after 3 months.

## Sprints
Sprints are 2 weeks long.

## Production data
Production database access is read-only and needs VP approval.
