## Rotation
On-call rotations run for one week, from Monday 10:00 to the next Monday 10:00.

## Allowance
The on-call allowance is $200 per week.

## Weekend pages
An engineer who is paged on a weekend gets a compensatory day off.

## Shift swaps
Shift swaps need 48 hours notice.
