## Public holidays
Acme gives 10 paid company holidays per year: 1 January, 26 January, 14 March, 1 May, 15 August, 2 October, 20 October, 8 November, 9 November and 25 December.

## Year-end shutdown
The office is closed from 26 December to 31 December. Shutdown days are not deducted from annual leave.
