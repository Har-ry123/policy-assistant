## Public holidays
Acme gives 10 paid company holidays per year: 1 January (New Year's Day), 26 January (Republic Day), 14 March (Holi), 1 May (Labour Day), 15 August (Independence Day), 2 October (Gandhi Jayanti), 20 October (Dussehra), 8 November (Diwali), 9 November (the day after Diwali) and 25 December (Christmas Day).

## Year-end shutdown
The office is closed from 26 December to 31 December. Shutdown days are not deducted from annual leave.
