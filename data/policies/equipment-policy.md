## Laptop refresh
Laptops are replaced every 3 years.

## Returning equipment
Return all company equipment within 5 working days of your last day.

## Peripheral allowance
Each employee gets a $150 peripheral allowance per year for items such as a keyboard or mouse.

## Personal use
Light personal use of the company laptop is allowed. Pirated software is never allowed.
