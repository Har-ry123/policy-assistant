## Probation
The probation period is 3 months. New joiners may take up to 3 days of leave during probation.

## Notice period
The notice period is 2 weeks during probation and 60 days after probation.

## Payroll
Salary is paid on the last working day of each month.

## Buddy
Every new joiner gets a buddy for the first week.
