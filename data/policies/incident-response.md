## Severity levels
A SEV1 incident means a full outage of the customer-facing product.

## Response time
For a SEV1 incident, the first response is due within 15 minutes.

## Status updates
Post a status page update every 30 minutes during a SEV1 incident.

## Reviews
A post-incident review must be held within 5 working days.
