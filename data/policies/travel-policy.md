## Booking
Book travel through the Acme Travel portal at least 14 days before departure.

## Flights
Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or more. Business class is allowed only for flights over 8 hours and needs VP approval.

## Hotels
Hotel cost is capped at $180 per night for domestic trips and $250 per night for international trips.

## Insurance
Travel insurance is added automatically for all business trips.

## Changes and cancellations
Cancel or change bookings at least 24 hours before departure to avoid fees.
