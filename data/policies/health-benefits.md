## Medical insurance
Medical insurance covers the employee, a spouse and up to 2 children. Cover starts on the first day of employment.

## Premiums
The company pays 100% of the premium for the employee and 50% of the premium for dependants.

## Hospital claims
Submit hospital claims within 45 days of discharge.

## Health check-up
Every employee gets one free health check-up per year.

## Counselling
The Employee Assistance Programme offers 6 free counselling sessions per year. Call 1800-555-0142 to book.
