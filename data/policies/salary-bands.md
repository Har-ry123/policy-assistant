## Bands by level
Level 1 pays $40,000 to $55,000. Level 2 pays $55,000 to $75,000. Level 3 pays $75,000 to $100,000. Level 4 pays $100,000 to $140,000.

## Bonus targets
Bonus target is 10% of salary for level 1 and level 2, 15% for level 3, and 20% for level 4.

## Visibility
These salary bands are confidential and visible to HR only.
